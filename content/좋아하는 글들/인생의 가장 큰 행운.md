> In English, they say, I am lucky to have you.  
> But in poetry, we say...  
> 
> Out of all the people this world has created,  
> with all the roads I could have taken,  
> and all the lives I could have lived,  
> some impossible miracle of timing allowed my story to cross paths with yours.
> 
> And if I spend the rest of my life searching for a word greater than lucky,  
> I still don't think it would be enough to explain what you are to me.

오글거릴 수도 있지만! 어찌하면 이렇게 아름다운 글을 쓸 수 있을까.
